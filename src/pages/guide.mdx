The editor is a remote control for your pedal. Every change you make on screen is sent to the pedal as a MIDI message,
so you hear it immediately.

**One thing to know first:** Chase Bliss pedals receive MIDI but don't send their state back. If you turn a knob on
the pedal itself, the editor doesn't notice. Use **Sync pedal** to send the editor's complete state to the pedal at
any time.

Connecting doesn't send anything, and the editor starts from its own defaults, not from what's on your pedal. MIDI
only goes out when you change something: a control you move sends just that one parameter, everything you don't
touch stays as it is on the pedal. To use your current sound as a starting point, set the editor's controls to the
same positions as on the pedal first.

## The pedal

- **Knobs:** drag up and down, scroll while a knob is focused, or use the arrow keys (hold Shift for fine steps).
  Double-click resets a knob to its default.
- **Toggles:** click a position. Some knob labels follow the toggles, just like on the pedal — for example the
  MOOD MKII shows what TIME and MODIFY do in the selected wet channel.
- **Footswitches:** click to switch, press and hold for the hold function (e.g. freeze and overdub on the
  MOOD MKII). The LEDs mimic the pedal.
- **Preset toggle:** left and right recall the pedal's two toggle presets, the middle position is live mode.

### Automatone pedals (Preamp MKII, CXM 1978, Big Time)

- **Faders:** drag the cap, scroll while a fader is focused, or use the arrow keys. The motorized faders on the
  pedal follow every move, so **Sync pedal** is also a nice way to see all six jump into place.
- **Arcade buttons:** click the button to step through its states like on the pedal, or click a state name
  directly. The LED shows the colour printed on the pedal.
- **Footswitches and display:** PRESET steps to the next preset, hold it for the next bank; hold BYPASS to save into
  the preset the editor last recalled. The display shows that preset, the BANK LED its bank. Big Time's footswitches
  change with MODE, and SHIFT swaps the faders and buttons for their alt functions.
- These pedals have no live mode and no preset toggle. Presets start at 0.

## Alt controls, hidden options and dip switches

Everything that is normally hidden behind both footswitches or the dip switches on the side of the pedal is right
there: alt knobs, hidden toggles, both dip switch banks and clock divisions. Some of these settings are stored
globally on the pedal — the editor marks them.

## Tools

- **Randomize** sends random settings for knobs, toggles, alt controls, ramping, or everything. A great way to find
  new sounds.
- **Editor defaults** brings everything back to the state the editor started with.
- **Tap** sends tap tempo to the pedal. Pedals that support it also accept a BPM value.
- **Factory reset** and **MIDI reset** (only where the pedal supports them) send the reset command to the pedal.

## Presets

- **In this browser:** give a preset a name and click **Save**. Click a preset to load it. Presets live in your
  browser's local storage — they stay until you clear the site data, and they are not shared between browsers.
- **On the pedal:** recall any of the 122 preset slots with a program change. Pedals with a MIDI save command
  (MOOD MKII, Generation Loss MKII, Reverse Mode C and the Automatone pedals) can store the current sound into a
  slot directly. On the others, hold both footswitches on the pedal and press **Recall** to save.

## MIDI monitor

Open the **MIDI monitor** below the pedal to see every message the editor sends. Handy if something doesn't react as
expected.
