My name is Marco, I'm from Munich, Germany. I'm a pedal nerd and I like software development :) I also play in a
death metal band called [UNINHIBITED](https://www.instagram.com/uninhibitedmusic/). You make me happy if you
follow :)

pedalzombie is an independent fan project and not affiliated with Chase Bliss. The editors are free and the source
code is open.

Feel free to contact me at **info [at] pedalzombie [dot] com** — I'm happy about feedback and always open for a chat.
