First things first: you need a **Chase Bliss pedal with a MIDI input** — one of the models listed on the
[home page](/). The editors here only send MIDI; without the pedal there is nothing to control.

Beyond the pedal you need four things: a supported browser, an interface with a MIDI output, a MIDI-to-TRS adapter
and a TRS cable.

<SetupChain />

## What you need

- **A Chase Bliss pedal** from the [list of supported pedals](/).
- **A browser that supports Web MIDI**, for example
  - Chrome (recommended)
  - Edge
  - Opera

  Safari and Firefox on iOS do not support Web MIDI.
- **An audio or MIDI interface with a MIDI out port**, for example the
  [UA Volt 2](https://www.uaudio.de/audio-interfaces/volt-2-usb.html).
- **A MIDI-to-TRS adapter**, for example
  - [Chase Bliss MIDIBox](https://www.chasebliss.eu/shoputility/p/midibox)
  - [Disaster Area MIDI Box](https://www.disasterareadesigns.com/shop/p/midi-box-4)
  - [Meris MIDI I/O](https://www.meris.us/product/midi-i-o/)
- **A TRS cable** from the adapter to the MIDI jack of your pedal.

Older pedals such as the Warped Vinyl MKII use the "ring active" wiring. Check the pedal's MIDI manual if your adapter
has a tip/ring switch.

### Automatone pedals: no adapter needed

Preamp MKII, CXM 1978 and Big Time have a regular **5-pin MIDI IN** socket. Run a standard 5-pin MIDI cable from the
MIDI OUT of your interface straight into the pedal; the MIDI-to-TRS adapter and the TRS cable are not needed. Their
MIDI THRU (Big Time: MIDI OUT) passes everything on to the next pedal in the chain.

## Connect your pedal

1. Plug everything in and open the editor for your pedal.
2. Click **Connect MIDI** and allow MIDI access when the browser asks.
3. Choose your interface under **Output**. The editor remembers it next time.
4. Choose the **MIDI channel** of your pedal. Most Chase Bliss pedals ship on channel 2; the older analog ones
   differ (Spectre 3, Gravitas 4, Warped Vinyl MKII 5, Warped Vinyl HiFi and Wombtone MKII 6). The editor starts on
   the pedal's default. To change the pedal's channel, hold both footswitches while powering it up — it then adopts the channel
   of the first program change it receives. On the Automatone pedals release the footswitches once the display
   lights up; CXM 1978 and Preamp MKII only listen for a program change here (use **Recall** in the editor), Big Time
   takes any message.
